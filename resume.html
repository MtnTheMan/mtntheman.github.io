Research and Technical Projects

Northern Hardwoods Resilience Project

2026–present

Doctoral Researcher and Research Lead · State of Practice and Management

  • Lead a range-wide assessment of relationships among written management intentions, implemented practices, and forest outcomes using qualitative interviews, management plans and records, policy and guidance documents, and field evidence across U.S. and Canadian jurisdictions.
  • Coordinate related workstreams on policy and management synthesis, silvicultural plans and post-treatment evidence, practitioner perspectives, forest economics, and resource forecasting and modeling.
  • Completed the 2026 U.S.–Canada field-travel phase and maintain reproducible, privacy-aware systems for evidence governance, qualitative analysis, image curation, and public field reporting.
  • Designed the Northern Hardwood Market Map v0.8, a twelve-state research preview comparing forest-products market access across approximately 5 × 5 km cells using public facility records, demand nodes, and capacity-constrained transportation allocation. Open the research preview.
  • Initiated the Northern Hardwood Pressure Atlas, a developing range-wide synthesis of biotic and abiotic factors that may help explain variation in management outcomes, including herbivory, invasive plants, pests and pathogens, drought, climate-related stress, and overlapping disturbance pressures.

Roads on Forest Service Lands: Their Genesis, Governance, and Guardianship

2026–present

Doctoral Researcher and Project Lead · Mixed-methods doctoral research in development

  • Designed a qualitative-dominant comparative study of road origins, legal and administrative status, public access, maintenance, data stewardship, and institutional memory across National Forest contexts.
  • Developed the study prospectus, literature-review framework, case-selection approach, interview protocol, evidence-verification process, and sequenced research plan.
  • Specified a terrain-mapping pilot that will integrate LiDAR and digital elevation models with archival records, historical imagery, field validation, manager knowledge, and administrative road data.

Lake States Agroforestry “Branching Out” Project

2023–present

Lead Researcher · Remote sensing, landowner decision-making, and survey methods

  • Mapped and characterized linear small woody features across 35 counties in Michigan, Wisconsin, and Ohio, then linked spatial evidence with parcel-level and two-wave landowner survey data to examine practice identification, adoption, and intentionality.
  • Rebuilt and audited the landowner-survey analysis workflow, documenting eligibility, linkage, attrition, missingness, diagnostics, sensitivity analyses, and integrity checks.
  • Developed an agroforestry-professionals survey instrument with multi-practice coverage, routing, scales, follow-up logic, and respondent-burden and accessibility review; the instrument remains under project review.

Michigan High-Resolution Land Cover and Woody Riparian Buffer Index

2025–2026

Project Lead and Workflow Designer · Statewide remote-sensing and ecological-indicator products

  • Developed a county-organized, 0.6-meter land-cover and canopy research product for all 83 Michigan counties from 2022 four-band NAIP imagery using a documented ArcGIS and PyTorch workflow with a U-Net and ResNet-34 encoder. Interactive map.
  • Designed a 0–1, bank-aware Woody Riparian Buffer Index using a 120-foot evaluation distance and distance decay. The preliminary statewide map is intended for exploratory screening of restoration priorities; validation and sensitivity analysis remain in progress. Interactive map.
